3 Benefits to incorporating Singing, Chanting & Toning

I spent 15 years as a childrens music and movement specialist.
It was here I reclaimed the joy of singing just for the sheer delight of it and my life has been ever enriched ever since.  You will always find me encouraging people to explore the power of their voice.


1. Vagus nerve. Sound vibrates it through the throat. Vagal tone is your off-switch for stress.


2. Respirar — to breathe, from spiritus, spirit. Same root. A held note stretches the exhale; a long exhale settles the body.


3. Emotional coherence. Feeling lives in throat and chest. Toning moves it without words.
