✦ Something to Keep ✦

Prayer for the Love Denied

A prayer came to me on a hand-lettered cloth. I don’t know who wrote it. It asks for the healing of the imprisoned, the unlearning of war, and it ends by handing the choice back: may we choose Life, for the good of all, according to free will.

I recorded it with music, filmed in the tunnel at Heart Creek Bunker. The page has the video, the words to read, and a photo of the cloth.
