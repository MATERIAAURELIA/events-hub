✦ FEATURED SOUND JOURNEY  ✦
The Power of your own voice...
People often ask me what my favourite sound healing instrument is.
I almost always say the human voice.    
In fact people often say your own voice is the most healing instrument for your own body and being.

As someone rooted in the practice of expressive arts therapy and facilitation, the power of generating sounds with our own human voice lies at the heart of my advocacy, and the invitation to a very powerful journey of inner transformation.
   
The Harmonic OM Journey will be our first  sounds journey that includes a collective, optional group vocal toning practice + guidance, within the evening.
 
My intention is for people to dip their toe's in and experience the power of vocal toning in a safe and encouraging place.   

To have an opportunity to feel the wonder and power of contributing your voices to the sound journey.
