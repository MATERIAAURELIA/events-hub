Soles of the Feet

standing · 2–3 min · from Thomas Hübl
GroundingReleasing tension
  1. Stand and bring your attention to the soles of your feet.
  2. Notice where your weight sits: the balls, the toes, the heels.
  3. Let it settle evenly, and notice what changes in your posture.

For feeling supported and present.

9 Purifying Breaths

seated or standing · 3–4 min · Tibetan Bön
ClearingCalmingFocusing
  1. Three rounds of three breaths, alternating nostrils.
  2. Breathe in clear, fresh air; pause gently.

Belly–Heart Breath

seated · 3–4 min
CalmingReleasing tension
  1. One hand on your belly, one on your heart.
  2. Breathe into the belly, then up into the heart.
  3. Let the out-breath be slow and soft.

For stress, anxiety and a busy mind.

Standing Like a Tree

standing · 3–5 min · Shaolin Zhan Zhuang
GroundingFocusingReleasing tension
  1. Stand with soft knees, feet hip-width apart.
  2. Round your arms as if hugging a tree.
  3. Breathe low and stay still. Let the tension drain down.

For a clear mind and steady focus.

Shake Out

standing · 1–2 min
EnergizingReleasing tension
  1. Shake your hands, then arms, shoulders and legs.
  2. Let the whole body shake loose for a minute.
  3. Stop, stand still, and feel the tingle.

4-7-8 Breath

seated · 2–3 min
Calming
  1. Breathe in through the nose for 4.
  2. Hold gently for 7.
  3. Breathe out slowly through the mouth for 8. Repeat four times.

For winding down, and for sleep.
