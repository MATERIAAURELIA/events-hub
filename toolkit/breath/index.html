Arm Swinging Qigong

standing · 3 min · from Shaolin · classic qigong (Shuai Shou Gong 甩手功)
GroundingEnergizingReleasing tension
  1. 1Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, knees soft.
  2. 2Turn from the waist and lower back (Ming Men), and let the arms swing loosely like empty sleeves.
  3. 3Let the hands tap the body as they swing: the front of the body, and the lower back over the kidneys.
  4. 4Breathe easy and keep it relaxed.

Kapalabhati

seated · 3 min · from Classical yoga · 'skull-shining breath'
ClearingEnergizingFocusing
The ideaClearing stagnant energy
  1. 1Sit tall, one hand on the belly.
  2. 2Snap the belly in for a short, strong exhale through the nose, then let go so the inhale happens on its own.
  3. 3Out is active, in is passive.
  4. 4Show 5 slow ones, then 20 at about one per second.
  5. 5Breathe all the way in, hold for a moment, release slowly and notice the tingle.
  6. 6Round two: 20 to 30, a little quicker if it feels good.
Good to knowSafety, always say it: stop if dizzy. Go gently or skip with pregnancy, high blood pressure, heart conditions or recent belly surgery. Keep the shoulders still; rhythm matters more than speed. In work settings keep rounds to 20 to 30. Cousin: Breath of Fire (Kundalini, equal active in and out).
