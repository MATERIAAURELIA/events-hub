A forest dance with music through wireless headphones — tuned to the land, water, sky and moon. We gather at 7:30 in the last golden light. Marcus guides us gently into movement — no steps to learn, just permission to move how the body wants — and we dance through dusk as the full moon rises through the trees.

Around 8:30 we come to rest in the grass, and the evening closes with a live sound healing transmission by Marcus — streamed straight into your headphones as you lie in the forest under the risen moon.
